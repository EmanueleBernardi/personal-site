Research

Research Interests

My research focuses on the mathematical modelling of complex systems, spanning biological, epidemiological, and socio-economic phenomena. It combines analytical methods and numerical simulations.Specific topics include:

  • Mathematical biology
  • Agent-based models
  • Kinetic theory
  • Multi-scale modelling
  • Integro-differential equations
  • Structured population models
  • Monte Carlo methods
  • Metaheuristics algorithms
